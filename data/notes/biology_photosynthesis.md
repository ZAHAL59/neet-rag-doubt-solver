# Photosynthesis in Higher Plants

## Overview
Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants use light energy to make glucose from carbon dioxide and water, releasing oxygen as a by-product. It takes place in the chloroplasts. The overall equation is 6CO2 + 12H2O, in the presence of light and chlorophyll, giving C6H12O6 + 6O2 + 6H2O.

## Light reaction
The light reaction, also called the photochemical phase, takes place in the thylakoid membranes of the grana. It includes light absorption, splitting of water (photolysis), release of oxygen, and formation of ATP and NADPH. The oxygen released in photosynthesis comes from water, not from carbon dioxide.

## Photosystems
Photosystem I has a reaction centre chlorophyll a that absorbs light best at 700 nm and is called P700. Photosystem II has a reaction centre that absorbs best at 680 nm and is called P680. In non-cyclic photophosphorylation both photosystems work together, whereas cyclic photophosphorylation uses only photosystem I and produces ATP but no NADPH.

## Calvin cycle (C3 pathway)
The Calvin cycle is the dark reaction or biosynthetic phase and takes place in the stroma. It has three stages: carboxylation, reduction and regeneration. In carboxylation, the enzyme RuBisCO fixes CO2 onto ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP), and the first stable product is 3-phosphoglyceric acid (3-PGA), a three-carbon compound. Making one molecule of glucose needs six turns of the cycle, 18 ATP and 12 NADPH.

## C4 pathway
C4 plants such as maize and sugarcane show Kranz anatomy, with bundle sheath cells around the vascular bundles. The first stable product is oxaloacetic acid (OAA), a four-carbon compound, and the primary CO2 acceptor is phosphoenolpyruvate (PEP), fixed by the enzyme PEP carboxylase. C4 plants avoid photorespiration and are more efficient at high temperature and light intensity.

## Photorespiration
Photorespiration happens in C3 plants when RuBisCO binds oxygen instead of carbon dioxide. It produces no ATP or sugar and wastes fixed carbon. It involves three organelles: the chloroplast, the peroxisome and the mitochondrion.
